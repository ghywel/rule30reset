Let N(n) be the number of words of length n that two adjacent columns of Rule 30 can read, over all rows on the integers. The topological entropy of the map is the growth rate of N(n) (`entropy_eq_pair_growth`), so each length k gives the upper bound log N(k)/k (`entropy_le_block_root`). A weighted reverse cover bounds all lengths at once (`ReverseCover.entropy_le`); a table of 140 states on five sites gives p/q = 1679211/714866, so the entropy is below 1.232039 bits a tick (`FiveSite.entropy_width_5`). Read beside one column, a pair word of columns -1 and 0 is a wall word with the OR of the wall and its eastern neighbour, so the entropy is the growth rate of these OR words summed over all walls (`HearingRate.entropy_eq_mask_growth`). Any bound on them uniform in the wall bounds the entropy (`HearingRate.entropy_le_of_mask`); the kit's per-wall bounds of Lowry and Galindo give 1.5 and 1.2854 bits. Every rational rate above the worst wall has a finite integer table, and one table bounds both the entropy and, under an eventual centre period, the single seed's horizontal words (`LookaheadHearing.strict_rate_has_certificate`, `LookaheadHearing.both_of_certificate`). The kit's `Mixing` has log 2 and log 3, and di Lena and Margara (2007) have a trace theorem of width three for permutive automata. This is about the full two-sided map: nothing is claimed about Problems 1 to 3, and whether the entropy is above one bit is open.
