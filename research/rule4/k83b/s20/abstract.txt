Let N(n) be the number of words of length n that two adjacent columns of Rule 30 can read, over all rows on the integers. The topological entropy of the map is the growth rate of N(n) (`entropy_eq_pair_growth`), so each single length k gives the upper bound log N(k) / k (`entropy_le_block_root`). A weighted reverse cover bounds all lengths at once: a finite table that reads the pair letters backwards and covers every physical word, with a positive integer weight at each state whose four successor weights sum to at most p/q times it, gives an entropy of at most log(p/q) (`ReverseCover.entropy_le`). A table of 140 states on a strip of five sites gives p/q = 1679211/714866, so the entropy is below 1.232039 bits a tick (`FiveSite.entropy_width_5`). The kit's `Mixing` has log 2 and log 3 as bounds, and its catalogue states the identity in words. Di Lena and Margara (2007) have a trace theorem of width three for permutive automata, and our notes of October 2026 have the two-column identity by hand. New here are the identity in Lean on Mathlib's cover entropy, the reusable bound and its instance. This is a statement about the full two-sided map and not about the centre column of one black cell: nothing is claimed about Problems 1 to 3, and whether the entropy is above one bit is open. Wider tables, checked outside Lean, give 1.144 bits.
