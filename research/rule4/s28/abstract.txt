For a prescribed T-tick Rule 30 boundary, the range of the driven right-half map has fair-coin measure 2^(-T), and the output law is fair measure conditioned on that range. The same identity holds for continuous feedback, in particular for the actual centre trace produced by any fixed left half. Almost every final right half recovers its finite past at and east of the boundary. For a fixed finite left half containing a black cell, the nullity of purely periodic centre words for every such left half suffices for almost-sure failure of eventual periodicity. For the black-first alternating boundary, a west of support at most B sustains alternation through tick T exactly when the required west is white at depths B+1 through T, and equality of required west prefixes is equivalent to equality of the corresponding gate prefixes. These statements are proved in Lean.
