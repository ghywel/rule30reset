**A stronger test for a proposed gate atom.** Let d(p) be the shortest length of an extension whose entire cylinder misses the target fibre. Along a target path begin at the empty prefix, and take disjoint blocks greedily, each of length d(p) at the current prefix p. Stop if a prefix already forces the target. At each step choose one shortest derailing extension, of conditional probability 2^(−d(p)). It cannot occur on the target fibre. Except on a null subset of the fibre one must then have Σ 2^(−d(p)) < ∞ along these successive prefixes. Indeed, on paths that have avoided the chosen branches, divide their survival indicator by the product of the successive conditional survival probabilities. This is a nonnegative martingale of mean one, hence has a finite limit almost surely. Where the sum diverges the product tends to zero, contradicting survival.
