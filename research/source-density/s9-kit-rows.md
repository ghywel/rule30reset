# S9: the rows for the kit's catalogue

Four rows for "Added in the kit" in the kit's `lean/README.md`, one line each. The builder fills `{DATE}` (the date of
the pull request), `{AUTHORS}` (the owner's form, as in our earlier rows), `{ID}` and `{PAGE}` (the portal entry).
`claudeCloud_s9_kit_form.py` copies every line that starts with a module cell.

| `Rule30SourceDensityRegion`, `Rule30SourceDensityCorners`, `Rule30DensitySharpness`, `Rule30DensityPolygon` | {DATE} | At a site of any Rule 30 history on the whole line, over any `n` consecutive ticks, let `C` count the ticks on which the site is black and `A` those among them on which its right neighbour is white (`black`, `ends`). Beside Condrey's `3C ≤ n + 5A + 3` of `Rule30SourceObservables`, every site and interval of every history has `2C ≤ n + 2A + 1` and `6C ≤ 5n + A + 5` (`middle_face`, `high_face`, `region`), by bounded potentials on two and on six cells, and one history attains the three additive constants (`optimal_startup`). Five histories periodic in space and time, on rings of 1, 12, 7, 155 and 2 cells, sit at the corners `(n, C, A) = (1,0,0), (3,1,0), (4,3,1), (10,9,4), (1,1,1)` (`corner0` to `corner4`). For integers `α`, `β`, `γ`, some constant `K` makes `α C + β A ≤ γ n + K` hold on every interval at every site of every history exactly when `0 ≤ γ`, `α ≤ 3γ`, `3α + β ≤ 4γ`, `9α + 4β ≤ 10γ` and `α + β ≤ γ`, which is the inequality without `K` at the five corners (`uniform_bound_iff`). `K = 9 α.natAbs + 4 β.natAbs` then suffices (`polygon_sufficient`). A nonzero finite seed can copy any number of periods of a corner history, so the same five conditions decide the question over nonzero finite seeds at site 0 from tick 0 (`polygon_necessary`, `finite_bound_iff`). Nothing here bounds the densities of the single seed. Builds on Condrey's `Rule30SourceObservables`, with its interval form by Quint and Gemini. Contributed by {AUTHORS} as Rule 30 Prize submission [{ID}]({PAGE}). | `region`, `optimal_startup`, `corner3`, `polygon_necessary`, `polygon_sufficient`, `uniform_bound_iff`, `finite_bound_iff` |
| `Rule30SourceTransitions` | {DATE} | In the counts of `Rule30SourceDensityRegion`, `C − A` at site `j` is the number of black-to-white steps of column `j + 1` over the same ticks, in every history (`fall_count`). Ulrey's `Rule30TwoBlockCounts` has this at the centre of the single seed, for one tick and as a count (`center_block_10_eq_left_black`, `count10_eq_spatialBlackLeftBlackCount`), and the two-block laws of Quint and Gemini contain the count. So `n − 2(C − A)` is the number of ticks at which column `j + 1` keeps its colour, plus a boundary term between −1 and 1 (`middle_identity`), and it is bounded above over all `n` exactly when column `j + 1` eventually alternates (`bounded_iff_alternates`). On the single seed, bounded error in the middle inequality at site `j` is eventual period two of column `j + 1` (`singleton_middle_bounded_iff_period_two`); at `j = −1` that column is the centre. Contributed by {AUTHORS} as Rule 30 Prize submission [{ID}]({PAGE}). | `fall_count`, `middle_identity`, `bounded_iff_alternates`, `singleton_middle_bounded_iff_period_two` |
| `Rule30DensityEscape`, `Rule30DensityMargin` | {DATE} | The single seed misses two corners of the region of `Rule30SourceDensityRegion` by a growing count. At site `j`, with `d 0 = j.natAbs` and `d (k+1) = 2 * d k + j.natAbs + 11`, every `n ≥ d k` has `2n + 7A − 5C ≥ k − 4` on the ticks `0` to `n − 1`, the distance in counts from the corner `(3/4, 1/4)` (`margin_three_quarters`); with 23 in place of 11, `6n + 3A − 8C ≥ k − 6`, the distance from `(9/10, 2/5)` (`margin_nine_tenths`). So the seed cannot follow either corner with bounded error, and the error grows at least like the logarithm of `n`. These are counts: the ratios `C/n` and `A/n` may still converge to the corner. A potential on six cells loses a nonnegative amount at every step, and a step without loss follows one of a short list of transitions (`range_potential`, `band_edge`). Six or twelve such steps in a row make columns `j` and `j + 1` repeat with period 4 or 10 (`tight_columns`). On the single seed that cannot last beside the moving left edge: from every tick `s ≥ j.natAbs` a loss occurs before tick `2s + j.natAbs + 11`, or `+ 23` for the second corner (`loss_in_interval`). Contributed by {AUTHORS} as Rule 30 Prize submission [{ID}]({PAGE}). | `margin_three_quarters`, `margin_nine_tenths`, `singleton_margin`, `tight_columns`, `loss_in_interval` |
| `Rule30DensityControl`, `Rule30DensityModes`, `Rule30DensityVertices`, `Rule30DensityOneHistory`, `Rule30DensityCornerHistories`, `Rule30DensityMixtures` | {DATE} | Every point of the region of `Rule30SourceDensityRegion` with rational coordinates is the limit of `(C/n, A/n)` along one history whose right half is arbitrary. Rule 30 is a bijection in its left input, so the left half can steer: any `W` cells at the sites `0` to `W − 1` can be installed in `W` ticks from every row (`steer_prefix`), and every stream of rows that obeys the rule at the sites `j ≥ 1` is the restriction of a full-line history (`realize_stream`, from `Rule30Realization`). A finite controller reads the cells `0` to `W − 1` and chooses the next bit of site 0 before it knows cell `W`; five kernel-checked tables (`zero_policy`, `oneThird_policy`, `threeQuarters_policy`, `nineTenths_policy`, `one_policy`) keep both counts within a fixed cap of a corner for ever (`block_bounds`, `physical_mode`). `corners_from_any_right_half`: from tick `W = 2, 6, 21, 37, 3` on, `D*C − c*n` and `D*A − a*n` stay within `0, 3, 3, 12, 0` of zero for the corners `(c/D, a/D) = (0,0), (1/3,0), (3/4,1/4), (9/10,2/5), (1,1)`. `schedule`: the controllers can be run in any order for any durations in one history, each after its `W` steering ticks. `mixtures_from_any_right_half`: with weights `w 0, …, w 4` of sum `K > 0` on the corners, some history with the prescribed right half has, for every `n` counted from tick 0, `(60*K*C − P*n)^2 ≤ 2*(K*(60*K+4317))^2*(n+1)` for `P = 20*w 1 + 45*w 2 + 54*w 3 + 60*w 4`, and the same for `A` with `P = 15*w 2 + 24*w 3 + 60*w 4`. Points with an irrational coordinate are not treated. Contributed by {AUTHORS} as Rule 30 Prize submission [{ID}]({PAGE}). | `corners_from_any_right_half`, `schedule`, `mixtures_from_any_right_half`, `physical_mode`, `steer_prefix` |
