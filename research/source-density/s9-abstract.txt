Watch one site of any Rule 30 history for T ticks. Let C count the ticks on which it is black, and A those among them on which its right neighbour is white. On every interval at every site, 3C ≤ T + 5A + 3, 2C ≤ T + 2A + 1 and 6C ≤ 5T + A + 5 (Rule30Submission.three_faces), and nothing more holds linearly: αC + βA ≤ γT + K holds for some K exactly when it holds with K = 0 at the five points (T, C, A) = (1,0,0), (3,1,0), (4,3,1), (10,9,4), (1,1,1) (Rule30Submission.linear_bound_iff), even over nonzero finite seeds. Five space-time periodic histories sit at these corners. From an arbitrary right half, each corner is tracked with bounded error, and every rational point of the pentagon is the limit of (C/T, A/T) along one history, with error O(1/√T) (Rule30Submission.every_rational_point). On the single seed the corners (3/4, 1/4) and (9/10, 2/5) are missed by a margin growing at least like log T, and bounded error in the middle inequality at a site is exactly eventual period two of the next column.
