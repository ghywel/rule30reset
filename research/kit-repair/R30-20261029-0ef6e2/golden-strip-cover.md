## A real-coordinate lemma checked at the portal pin

Write $d=\sqrt5$, $g=d-1$, $v=9-4d$. The word is $c_\theta(t)=\lfloor\theta+tg\rfloor\bmod2$. At every scale $n\ge3$ and every real phase, the N9 hand argument gives an agreement interval $[a,b]$ at a positive lag $q$ with $b-2a-q\ge0.0999q-3$. The lags in question grow uniformly with $n$. ## From the cover to repetitions

For a lattice point $P=(a,b)$ put $V(P)=a+bd$, $U(P)=-a+bd$. In the N9 scale-$n$ torus take a representative $(x,y)\in[0,1)^2$. Translate $x$ by the integer $-\lfloor x+yd\rfloor$, moving the affine lattice by one of its periods. Then $s=x+yd\in[0,1)$ and $z=-x+yd=2yd-s\in(-1,5)$. The coordinates of $P$ are $W_P=s+V(P)$ and $z+U(P)$.

1. The six possible base displacements $e$ have pairs $(-3,1),(-4,2),(-7,3),(-11,5),(-18,8),(-29,13)$. Each has $|V(e)|<1$, $Q=U(e)>1$, and even $e.a+e.b$. Multiplication by $v^n$ is an integer lattice automorphism equal to the identity modulo 2. Write $v^n V(e)=qg-P_0$ with $P_0$ even. Then $2dv^n q=Q+h V(e)$ where $h=v^{2n}$. In particular $q$ tends uniformly to infinity over the six families.
2. A point is a mismatch exactly when $W_P\in[\min(0,-V(e)),\max(0,-V(e)))$. Its signed integer time satisfies $2dv^n t_P=z+U(P)+h W_P$. For active points the width is less than one, so there is at most one integer $a$ at a given $b$. The difference in scaled times between two active points is $2d\,\Delta b-(1-h)\Delta W$. Since $|\Delta W|<1$ and $0<h<1$, increasing $b$ is the exact time order, including negative times.
3. On a slab $l\le s<r$, the Lean table verifies membership of each witness endpoint for the whole slab. For every intermediate integer height $b$ it supplies an integer $k$ with $r+k-1+bd\le\min(0,-V(e))$ and $\max(0,-V(e))\le l+k+bd$. Every integer at this height misses the strip. Each test is an affine inequality in $d$. Checking it at both rational endpoints $2236067/10^6$ and $2236069/10^6$ proves it at $\sqrt5$.
4. For consecutive active points $A,B$, the cover gives $z+U(B)\ge1.1Q$ and $U(B)-2U(A)-z\ge1.1Q$. Their intersection is a **closed interval** of $z$. Thus the bounds apply whether $t_A$ precedes or follows time zero. In the exact scaled inequalities the maximum loss from the $h$ terms is $(3+1.0999)h$, since $|W_A|,|W_B|,|V(e)|\le1$. Here $v<1/16$, $h\le v^6<1/16^6$, and $0.0001Q>(3+1.0999)h$. Consequently both $t_B$ and $t_B-2t_A$ are at least $1.0999q$.
5. The agreement starts at $a=\max(0,t_A+1)$ and ends at $b=t_B-1$. These two bounds give $b-2a-q\ge0.0999q-3$. Dropping a fixed number of initial ticks costs at most twice that cutoff in the excess. The lags and the excess therefore remain unbounded under any fixed visible-word transient.
