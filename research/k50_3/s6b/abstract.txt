In core Lean 4.35.0 we prove the kit's inverse-tick theorems on the whole line, without the white wall, and show that both of Hughes's ages are sharp there. Let `x : Int → Bool` have a leftmost black cell at `lo`, evolved by rule 30 on the whole line. From age 8, every predecessor of `evol x t` has its leftmost black cell at `lo - t + 1` (`Rule30Submission.predecessor_edge_from_age_eight`), and age 7 does not suffice (`Rule30Submission.predecessor_edge_fails_at_age_seven`). From age 24, every two-step predecessor has it at `lo - t + 2` (`Rule30Submission.two_step_predecessor_edge_from_age_twenty_four`), and age 23 does not suffice: black cells at 0, 3, 4, 6, 7, 10, 11, 12 and 14 have, at age 23, a two-step predecessor of their image with black cells arbitrarily far to the left (`Rule30Submission.two_step_predecessor_edge_fails_at_age_twenty_three`). So 8 and 24 are the least ages (`Rule30Submission.one_tick_least_age`, `Rule30Submission.two_tick_least_age`). For every k, the kit's reset capacity `CAP` gives an age from which every k-tick predecessor has it at `lo - t + k` (`Rule30Submission.every_k_line_of_cap`), and so does the relaxed doubling lemma (`Rule30Submission.every_k_line_of_dlMinusOne`). The kit proves these statements beside the white wall, for finite rows of the zero-boundary left half. On the line the band of cells next to the edge is always full, so the narrow-row cases disappear. Nothing is assumed of the predecessors. `CAP` and the relaxed lemma are open, and nothing here concerns the centre column.
