Sample the cell immediately to the right of an alternating Rule 30 column at odd ticks. After the first sixteen cells enter Bosdure's gate trap, successive black samples have gaps of three or five. Consider a run of four consecutive 5-gaps bounded by 3-gaps. We prove that four such closed runs in succession cannot begin two or more samples after a trapped tick. The delay is sharp. One explicit finite right half produces four closed runs one sample after a trapped tick, and another produces three closed runs sixty samples later. We also exclude five consecutive blocks of four 5-gaps followed by a 3-gap, starting anywhere from the trapped tick onward. Lean checks the exclusions by propagating possible sample times through a finite tree of spatial prefixes. The same source checks both physical witnesses.
