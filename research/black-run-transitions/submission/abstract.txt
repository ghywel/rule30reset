In core Lean 4.35.0 we extend Katip Kurtuluş's run-transition theorem for rule 30, the kit's `Rule30RunTransitions`, from the centre column of the single seed to every column of every configuration with a leftmost black cell, and add sharp two-run windows. Suppose a column reads a black run of length `a`, one white, then a black run of length `r ≥ 1`. The right-hand neighbour at the next tick then has the same colour in every row exactly when `a` reaches a cutoff: 8 for `r` = 1, 2, 3 or 5; 15 for `r = 4`; 16 for `r = 6`; 4 for `r = 7`; 14 for `r = 8`; and 0 for `r ≥ 9` (`Rule30Submission.exact_pair`, with the colour in `Rule30Submission.pair_forces`). Below each cutoff both colours occur at any prescribed site, even from a finite row (`Rule30Submission.both_colours_below`). At each white letter of an eventual period, one of Kurtuluş's windows (`Rule30Submission.old_forcing_window`) or one of these pair windows may be used, chosen letter by letter. If every white letter has one, no column of any configuration with a leftmost black cell eventually repeats the word, whatever the transient (`Rule30Submission.not_eventually_combined`, from the general form `Rule30Submission.exclude_of_windows`). One theorem covers every period length. The cycle of black runs 16, 4, 9, 1, 4, 9, each followed by one white, needs both kinds (`Rule30Submission.mixedExample_requires_both`). Nothing here decides the centre column.
