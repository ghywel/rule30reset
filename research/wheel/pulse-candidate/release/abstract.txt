Suppose a Rule 30 column alternates, starting black. At each white tick, observe its immediate right neighbour. When this neighbour is black, how long until it is black at a white tick again? From the second white tick onward, two simultaneous measurements give the exact answer on every right half. Read sites 2 and 5 to the right of the alternating column. With white encoded as 0 and black as 1, the pairs 00, 01, 10 and 11 give returns after 8, 4, 10 and 6 physical ticks respectively. All four occur on finite initial rows at the same tick, and a finite example shows that the first white tick can give a different answer. Applied to the single-black-cell initial condition, an early or missing return places a failure of the proposed centre alternation strictly before the predicted endpoint. This gives a finite test for Problem 1. Eventual period two remains open.
