# Kernel checks for the single-bit trap observer

All sample numbers in this note refer to white ticks of the alternating column. A sample reads only the positive neighbour at site 1. The trap predicate refers to all 16 positive cells.

* [WheelGateObserver](WheelGateObserver.lean) defines `Clean` as the absence of five whole words, `00000`, `11`, `101`, `10001` and `1001001`, inside the measured block. Its executable scan has that specification. Given a proof that a clean window of N samples forces the last state into the trap, the kit’s gap and coverage theorems imply that every entry is detected. A trajectory remains outside forever exactly when **every** N-sample block contains a forbidden word. The finite-right dichotomy RD then asks whether each finite right start with an aperiodic gate eventually has some clean block. [Stock receipt](data/gate-observer-stock.json).
* [WheelObserverLowerBound](WheelObserverLowerBound.lean) proves an unconditional lower bound for **every deterministic function of at most 47 consecutive gate bits**. The exterior seed `0xa224d3200017fb5ccd115f23a59` and the interior seed `0x4319fc9fcd612848b70077729985e4b9` are physical finite rows, with site 1 in the low bit and the wall black at time zero. The first window starts at sample 0 and ends outside at 46. The second starts at 10 after entry at 0 and ends inside at 56. They read exactly the same 47 bits. Every shorter suffix keeps those two incompatible endpoints. Lean checks the two labels, finiteness, all 47 physical readings, and the universal classifier statement. A classifier that also receives the initial spatial row or a longer history has different inputs. [Stock receipt](data/observer-lower-stock.json).
* [WheelObserverCleanup](WheelObserverCleanup.lean) proves that **all** forbidden factors cease to start six samples after entry. The kit’s gap and black-coverage theorems give the first four exclusions from entry. For `1001001`, just six of the 829 trap states can start the word. The successive sets that can reach one of those six in exactly one to six sampled updates have sizes 4, 4, 3, 2, 1 and 0. The checker validates a backward implication on each of the four allowed eastern boundary choices at every trap state. The finite row `0x7fffff80fc` enters at sample 0 and begins `1001001` at sample 5. Thus six is the least uniform cleanup delay. [Stock receipt](data/observer-cleanup-stock.json).

The 48-sample rolling filter has a **sharp worst-case first-confirmation delay of 53 white intervals**, or 106 physical updates, when observations begin at entry. For the same finite row the windows starting at samples 0 through 5 all contain the forbidden factor at samples 5 through 11. The first passing window spans samples 6 through 53. For every row entering at sample `a`, the filter accepts the window ending at `a+53` and every later window. The theorem `first_confirmation_53` gives the finite sharpness witness and `uniform_confirmation_delay_iff` shows that a deadline `d` works for all entries exactly when `53 ≤ d`. Both statements concern the five-word filter itself. [Lean proof](WheelObserverCleanup.lean) · [Stock receipt](data/observer-cleanup-stock.json).
