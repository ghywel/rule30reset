# K92.5 · Which outside arguments reach the Rule 30 seed?

**The closest transfer to the prize is a counting question at the white wall.** Let S(B,k) count the finite starts below 2ᴮ that are black next to the wall at each of the first k even ticks, including tick zero. Q01, the assertion that every finite start eventually fails this check, is equivalent to the deficit **2048B − S(B,k) being arbitrarily large**, with B and k free to vary. This arithmetic statement is checked in Lean. It leaves a concrete target in K92.2's notation: prove **A(n,n) ≤ 4095n at unbounded n**. Such a bound allows surviving rows at every tested width. It would exclude an eventually alternating prize column. [Census proof and credits](arithmetic-transfer.md) · [A(k,n) conventions](../ladder/sources.md)

## A front where the phase at the wall decides the answer

PROVED IN LEAN. Take one Rule 30 tick followed by one Rule 180 tick and look at a row with no black cells west of L. The first tick can create a black cell at L−1. If it does, it also makes L black, and Rule 180 clears L−1 on the second tick. The two-tick update therefore preserves the white half-line. If L starts black, the pair leaves it black precisely when both of the next two cells east were white. In that case at least one of those two neighbours is black after the pair: if the original L+3 was black the new L+1 is black, and otherwise the new L+2 is black. Two successive stalls are impossible. By tick 4n all sites below L+n are white. On the full line both temporal orders eventually whiten every fixed site, for an arbitrary infinite east. [Physical induction](HybridWallFront.lean)

Clamp site 0 white at each tick. The same four-tick proof applies at every left edge L≤−4. From a west of width W it reaches the last three sites after at most 4·max(W−3,0) ticks. The remaining exact two-tick map on the packed rows 0 through 7 is **[0,0,2,1,5,3,3,2]**. Every row has a white reading at depth one within three further pairs, counting its entrance tick. The resulting inclusive failure deadline is **4·max(W−3,0)+6**. Reversing the rule order gives **7→2→7** forever, black at every even tick. Both orders wash a finite start past every fixed site on the full line. The imposed boundary and its phase decide which wall theorem holds. [Lean proof](HybridWallFront.lean) · [Eight terminal trajectories](hybrid_terminal.py)

PROVED IN LEAN. The other instructive control is temporal (30,150). On a row supported entirely on one spatial parity, the Rule 30 quadratic term vanishes. Two Rule 150 ticks preserve that support parity because the three inputs to the squared linear rule are spaced by two. The whole hybrid orbit therefore agrees with Rule 150 on every such start. If the start is also reflection-symmetric about zero, its centre keeps its initial colour at **every tick**, in particular black for the singleton. [Parity proof and flashing-rule conventions](HybridParity.lean)

## What the ternary square root tells us about the actual seed

Wolfram's NKS note names the codes 11736, 11739 and 11742. The kit catalogue cites them as unchecked. COMPUTED: they exhaust all 3⁹ ordered nearest-pair tables with the particular embedding white=0 and black=2, index 3a+b in increasing west-to-east order. This count fixes the embedding and the lattice orientation. PROVED IN LEAN: for each code the odd half-step at the bond from i to i+1 reads **0 for 00, 2 for 01, and 1 for 1\***. The next half-step combines the two bonds touching i and returns the encoded physical Rule 30 value at i. Iteration works on every binary row. Intermediate rows are exactly the ternary rows avoiding the three pairs 01, 20 and 22. The three codes differ only on 01, a pair that never occurs at the intermediate stage. [Wolfram, NKS p. 956](https://www.wolframscience.com/nks/notes-6-6--square-root-of-rule-30/) · [Exhaustive check](ternary_roots.py) · [Core Lean](TernaryRoot.lean)

A fixed intermediate bond is **never eventually periodic** for a row with a leftmost black cell, including the actual singleton. Its 1-indicator reads the left cell of the bond and its nonzero-indicator reads the OR of its two endpoints. Those are exactly the two observables in the kit's masked Jen theorem. One tick of lookahead recovers the west neighbour. Thus the square root gives a one-site ternary presentation of a known obstruction. Since any two colour indicators determine the third, at least two of the three binary readings 00, 01 and 1* are themselves aperiodic at each bond (kernel-checked, allowing different candidate periods). The one-bit projection “is the bond colour 1?” at the origin is exactly the prize column and remains open. [Finite masked Jen](https://github.com/WolframInstitute/Rule30PrizeKit/blob/ea5d70e/lean/Ruliology/CellularAutomata/Rule30FiniteMaskedJen.lean) · [Decoder and fixed-bond theorem](TernaryRoot.lean)

## Decision for Cloud

There is also a named cost benchmark on the actual seed. Read its centre at even indices 2t and the OR of the centre and site 1 at odd indices 2t+1, both from physical tick t. For each correct lookup machine and every k≥1, some input in [2ᵏ⁻¹, 3·2ᵏ⁻¹) takes at least k+1 steps and reaches tape cell k. A period of this interleaved sequence would make both readings eventually periodic, contrary to masked Jen. The cost of looking up just the centre is still open beyond the kit's bounds. [Machine proof](ColumnCost.lean)

One shortcut is now ruled out. The 4,096 ancestors at any fixed eligible width all merge at tick 24. Twelve free odd-time readings distinguish every surviving preimage of that endpoint, so the published construction already occupies all 4,096 places. Composing inverse histories of depth at most 24 with nonnegative width increments still leads to the same 24-tick endpoint at each width. A larger lower bound needs distinct endpoints or further free readings. [Exact fibre and splice invariant](HistoryFibre.lean)

The same observation works as an upper-bound test for K92.2. Among the finite starts counted by A(n,n), group by their exact row **at tick 24**, for n≥6. They have passed all twelve even checks through tick 22. Each group has at most 4,096 members, since the endpoint and the twelve odd readings determine the start. If the number E(n) of different endpoints has unbounded deficit n−E(n), then 4096n−A(n,n) is also unbounded, giving Q01. This counts the images of the final survivors at a fixed early time. Counting their images only at the growing final observation time would need a different bound on preimage multiplicity. [Finite-history uniqueness](HistoryFibre.lean) · [Census criterion](WindowCensus.lean)

Keep K92.5 in progress for one specific transfer. Try to bound A(n,n) on an unbounded sequence by counting white blocks of length 2n−1 after the support in the sideways free-word representation. First establish an upper estimate that remains valid after an earlier white run. The diagonal is a convenient first target. If the time scale is too short, A(k(n),n)≤4095n at unbounded n is equally sufficient for any chosen number k(n) of post-support checks. The known first- and second-check estimates have no such conditional iteration. Treat the hybrid theorem as a possible kit control, subject to a different-model research read and a fit check.
