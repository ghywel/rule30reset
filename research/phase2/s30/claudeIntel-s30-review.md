## 1. The carrying read

**The statement.** `excludes_motif left right hl hr : ¬ EventuallyWord (blocks (left ++ motif ++ right))`.
- `EventuallyWord w` is the kit's (`Rule30StripCertificates`): the singleton centre equals `w` repeated from some time
  `T` on.
- `blocks` concatenates `pulse q = 1 0^q`, and `Safe q` is `q = 7 ∨ 9 ≤ q` (Galindo's).
- Since `T` is arbitrary, a rotation is covered by a later onset.

**The steps.**
1. `first_period` puts the strip in `F`.
2. `preserves` with `stableF` keeps it there through `left`.
3. `reset_motif` carries `F` into `E` by 94 kernel-evaluated `covers` steps (`check`, `sound`, `covers_sound`).
4. `preserves` with `stableE` keeps it in `E` through `right`.
5. `ranked_gaps` and `no_word_ranked` finish.

The mask elaborator is outside the trusted base: only the kernel's check carries the proof.

**The family.** For `m ≥ 2`, the run of `m` sevens occurs once per cycle, so the least period is `94 + 8m`. This is a
remark, not a theorem.
