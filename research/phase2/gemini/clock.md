# Q3 · How long can a first clock loss go without a reset?

**Every five consecutive ticks contain an isolated black cell in the first white interval, at every dyadic clock.** If the old clock first fails after the permanently white wheel a, and b is the next permanently white wheel, the whole spatial word `010` at that tick lies between a and min(b,a+7), inclusive. There is a second guarantee within min(b,a+6). For every three consecutive old phases, one of their six readings across the two halves of the doubled clock contains `010`. Both bounds hold from any starting tick, including zero. [Lean proofs](ClockPhasePairs.lean)

For a fixed first loss, the five-tick theorem gives at least ⌊N/5⌋ distinct ticks with a singleton reset in any N successive ticks, by partition into disjoint blocks of five. On a black-edged recurrent strip, one *fixed* member of any five consecutive ticks sees such singletons arbitrarily far along the strip. Each occurs at most seven wheels past a first loss. To see this, take first losses beyond any given depth and apply the five-tick theorem there. If each of the five ticks had a last such occurrence, a first loss beyond all five would contradict the theorem. This corollary is kernel checked as `cofinal_singletons`. R44 had already put some closed run of length 1 modulo 3 at arbitrarily large depths on a fixed one of three ticks. Here the run length equals one and its position has a bound relative to the specified first loss. [R44](https://github.com/WolframInstitute/Rule30PrizeKit/blob/ea5d70e/lean/Ruliology/CellularAutomata/Rule30RecurrentStrip.lean)

## Why the singleton appears

Number the wheels outward from a. Let x be wheel 1, the first one to lose the old clock P = 2ᵉ. The kit proves that wheel 2 is permanently black, wheel 3 reads the complement of the previous x, and x(t+P) is the complement of x(t). A fall of x therefore writes `010` on wheels 1–3 at the arrival tick. [First-loss cascade](https://github.com/WolframInstitute/Rule30PrizeKit/blob/ea5d70e/lean/Ruliology/CellularAutomata/Rule30RelaxedWindows.lean)

The remaining cases use the same three-cell calculation. If a white wheel has a toggling neighbour on its right for two updates, that neighbour falls on one of them. If the next wheel is white before the fall, the toggling one is already isolated. If the next wheel is black, Rule 30 leaves it black and forces the wheel beyond it white on the next tick. This gives `010` one tick later and one wheel farther out. The black witnesses exclude a permanently white interval end strictly inside the claimed motif.

* Two consecutive black readings of x make wheel 3 white for two ticks. Wheel 4 toggles on both updates. One of the next three ticks has a singleton by wheel 6.
* Three consecutive white readings of x make wheel 3 black, then wheel 4 white and wheel 5 toggle. One of ticks two through four measured from the start has a singleton by wheel 7.
* Among any four driver bits there is a fall, or the first three are white, or the last two are black. Applying the preceding cases gives a singleton in the next five ticks. Prefix recurrence translates the argument back through tick zero.
* For the paired result, any change in four driver bits is a fall in one of the two complementary clock halves. With no change, one half has two consecutive black readings. This proves the three-pair bound by wheel 6.

## Registered test and the remaining obstruction

I318 registered a relaxed local test before execution. A simultaneous miss at a tick and its P-translate requires the driver to have the same value as on the preceding tick, since any change gives a falling half. For r consecutive pairs this fixes a suffix of r+1 driver bits. Exchange the two halves to normalize that suffix to zero. Independently choose the initial bit of each deeper wheel in the two halves, enforce the Rule 30 update on the temporal window, and reject when a white boundary closes a black run of length 1 modulo 3 on a watched tick. Independence enlarges the set of possible physical strips. [Registration I318](https://github.com/ghywel/rule30reset/commit/f733f85) · [Frozen test](clock_phase_pairs.py)
