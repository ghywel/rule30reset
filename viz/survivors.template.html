Survivors at the Wall

Put a permanently white wall beside a finite row of rule 30 cells and run it. The cell next to the wall is black at every even tick for a while, and then it is not. The open statement Q01, which would exclude period 2 for the centre column, says this always happens. These pictures show how long rows last, which rows last longest, and what the constraint looks like as algebra.

F(Y)
One rule 30 step beside the wall: (Y >> 1) xor (Y or (Y << 1)). Bit i of Y is the cell at depth i+1 from the wall (kit Rule30ZeroBoundary.IntegerForm.F).
G = F∘F
Two steps. Q01 holds exactly when every natural number Y reaches an even number under G (kit Q01_iff_integer).
σ(Y)
The stopping time: the least s with Gs(Y) even. A row with σ = s keeps depth 1 black at ticks 0, 2, …, 2s−2 and loses it at tick 2s.
Theta(f)
The constrained start whose depth-1 cell reads 1, f0, 1, f1, … (kit Theta). Q01 says no Theta(f) is a finite row (kit Q01_iff_Theta).

One pixel per row Y, 1024 × 1024 of them, coloured by σ(Y). In natural order Y = 1024·line + column. In 2-adic order the bits are reversed, so rows that agree near the wall sit together: the low bits pick the line and the high bits the column.

What to look for σ ≥ k depends only on the lowest 2k − 1 bits, so in 2-adic order each set σ ≥ k is a union of whole blocks: exactly 2k−1 of the 22k−1 blocks of its size, one for each word of free bits. That part is exact and known. The finer grain, where a row's zeros above its top bit begin to matter, is where the rows that live longest sit.

The constrained start as a polynomial

The cell of Theta(f) at depth d is a polynomial over GF(2) in the free bits f0 … f⌊d/2⌋−1. The newest free bit enters each even depth on its own, so even depths are exactly balanced. An odd depth brings no new free bit: it is a pure constraint on the bits already chosen. Q01 asks whether these constraints can all hold at zero from some depth on. The degree at odd depths is known: depth 2n+1 is the kit's first-check polynomial, of degree n for odd n and n−1 for even n (Rule30FirstCheckDegree.check_degree), and the boundary of linearity at depth 7 is Rule30LinearCharts.

The monomials of one depth

One cell per possible monomial; filled if present. The top-left cell is the constant term. Moving right adds the low free bits f0, f1, …; moving down adds the high ones. A monomial set closed under taking subsets would draw a Sierpiński-like triangle.

Where the numbers come from. Two generators in this folder, both exact: gen_survival.py computes σ(Y) for all 224 rows Y below 224 with 128-bit arithmetic, rechecks every champion and 2000 random rows with Python integers, and wrote the data with SHA-256 a867c1cc…. gen_theta_anf.py builds Theta over all 220 free words by sideways reconstruction, replays 4096 of them forward through F, and takes the Möbius transform at each depth (SHA-256 577a64bc…). Both ran on the M5 laptop in about a second each, under memory caps. The predictions were written down before each run, in PREDICTIONS.md, with what they got right and wrong.
